The questionExplore a responseChoose a readoutNext experiment

The Schjerven Lab studies how IKAROS controls immune-cell identity and how its disruption contributes to leukemia. Its recent B-ALL study links IKAROS restoration to an ERG-dependent regulatory program. A practical follow-up question is: which early RNA responses are consistent enough across models to take into a validation experiment?

I built an explorer of the study’s eight deposited RNA-seq libraries to separate shared responses from signals that depend on the model, replicate or normalization. Start with ERG, then choose a small panel of candidate readouts with the underlying measurements still in view.

How it works

Compare IK1 induction with GFP control after 24 hours in MXP5 and PDX2, with two biological replicates per condition. Each model is compared with its own control. PDX2 was collected in an earlier study, so differences between models may also reflect sample preparation; this is not an independent validation cohort.

Analysis 2 — Choose a readout to test

Decision
How much of the shortlist survives the replicate check?

A mean-only result can be useful for discovery. For a follow-up measurement, it is also useful to ask whether the smallest observed response clears a chosen effect floor in both models. These are adjustable selection rules, not validated significance or quality cutoffs.

What the sample-level check can—and cannot—tell us

This adapts the Pearson-correlation component of ssvQC, a Schjerven-coauthored collaborative project, to these gene counts. Raw counts can be dominated by highly expressed genes; toggle to log-CPM to see the sensitivity. High correlation does not establish sample quality or remove batch effects. No sample is excluded here.

Why this matters for the lab

The preprint already identifies ERG as a vulnerability. This analysis asks a narrower question: which measured transcripts are sensible readouts of the early response, and which require more context before interpreting a follow-up assay? A shared expression change is not evidence that a gene is a therapeutic target or a direct IKAROS target.

Proposed follow-up design and recording sheet

In fresh, independent cultures, repeat IK1 and GFP induction within each model with matched processing. Confirm IK1 protein induction and record viability/growth. Test a small transcript panel by RT-qPCR only after validating reference genes and primer efficiency. Earlier and later time points could help distinguish an early regulatory response from a change accompanying growth arrest.

Choose biological replicate numbers through a prospective power/variance assessment. Do not treat technical wells as independent cultures. The exported record leaves unmeasured fields blank and separates the existing RNA evidence from proposed qPCR/protein measurements.

Methods note

Source: GSE310958, associated with Paculova et al., 2025 preprint (not peer reviewed at the source checked). Eight libraries; 60,669 deposited rows aggregated by exact Ensembl ID into 60,624 genes. Symbols come from GENCODE v36. PDX2 originates from GSE90659 and is reprocessed here, not a new independent cohort.

Normalization, selection and reproducibility

CPM divides each gene’s count by the sum of assigned gene counts in its library, then multiplies by one million; this is not a sequenced-read depth. Median-ratio factors are estimated separately within each model using genes positive in all four samples, then scaled to geometric mean one. The effect is log₂(mean normalized IK1 / mean normalized GFP), without a pseudocount. Zero condition means are undefined; any zero individual value excludes that gene from the strict rule.

The expression floor applies to the mean CPM in all four model/condition groups, regardless of effect normalization. Both mean effects must clear the effect floor and share a direction. The strict rule additionally requires all four unpaired sample comparisons in each model to clear that floor in the same direction. The shortlist is descriptive: no p-values, false-discovery estimates or DESeq2 result reproduction are claimed.

The paper’s common-effect model and this model-stratified inspection answer different questions; this is not a criticism of its statistical analysis. With n = 2, model/preparation confounding and one time point, this cannot establish direct regulation, protein abundance, drug sensitivity or a causal mechanism. Gene-level IKZF1 counts do not separate endogenous, mutant and induced isoforms.

The repository contains a pinned source manifest, deterministic data build, an attributed and tested Python port of the ssvQC Pearson component, and analysis tests. It does not run the full ssvQC R package. Implementation was assistant-supported; this artifact does not establish unaided programming or hands-on experience with the lab’s experimental techniques.
